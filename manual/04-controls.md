# Controls

Everything is on the keyboard. The keys work the same in a terminal and in the app.

## On foot

These are the keys on the map. The `?` key list shows the same ones while you play.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `hjkl yubn ↑↓←→` | move; `yubn` go diagonally |
| `< >` | go down or up: stairs, a lift, a ladder, a manhole, a hub |
| `W` | up to the overworld from the open street; jacked in, the Net over the world |
| `space` | the thing in reach: look, talk, take, jack in. On turns, stay where you are |
| `x` | look around with a cursor |
| `1-5` | fire the ability in that slot; jacked in, pick a program |
| `a` | every ability you have, and whether it's ready |
| `J` | jack out (it asks first) |
| `e` | go to turns now; again to go back |
| `.` | end your turn; jacked in, hurry the beat |
| `g` | on turns, aim a walk; `enter` walks it |
| `tab` | the deck |
| `F1-F4` | open or close a pane |
| `v` | the panes as a popup |
| `m` | the whole log |
| `d` | the digital overlay |
| `s` | save |
| `?` | every key on one card |
| `esc q` | the pause menu |

> Moving into someone on the street trades places with them. Moving into someone who wants you dead doesn't.

## The thing in reach

`space` acts on what is next to you. With one thing in reach, its window opens: a door, a terminal, a person, a bed. With nothing there you get a look at where you stand. With several, the game asks which way you mean, and you pick with the arrows.

A window lists what you can do, each with a letter: `t Talk`, `j Jack in`, `i Interact`. Press the letter, or move to the row and press `space`. `esc` backs out of any window.

## Looking around

`x` puts a cursor on the map. Move it with the arrows or `hjkl yubn`, and hold shift to move it eight tiles at a time. `tab` jumps it to the next thing worth a look, and `space` opens what it's on if that's in reach. `esc` or `x` puts it away.

## On turns

When something hunts you, the map goes to turns (Turns and fights). Your turn comes in two halves:

- **Moving**: step with the movement keys, or press `g`, aim with the arrows and press `enter` to walk the whole path. `space` stays where you are and moves you on to acting.
- **Acting**: the cursor comes up on your target. `t` jumps to the next one, `space` fires, and `1` to `9` (and `0`) pick an ability or something to throw. `.` ends your turn.

While someone else takes their turn, the game waits for them. You can still read the log, look around and open the pause menu.

## Menus and windows

In menus `↑↓` move and `enter` chooses; in the game's windows and popups `space` confirms. `esc` always backs out one step.

The pause menu (`esc` on the map) has:

- `RESUME`, back to the game.
- `SAVE GAME`, which saves where you stand.
- `LOAD GAME`, which saves, then lists your runs.
- `SETTINGS`, below.
- `MANUAL`, this booklet.
- `QUIT TO TITLE` and `QUIT GAME`, which both save first.

### Settings

| Setting | What it changes |
|---|---|
| Rain | the weather over the strata |
| Cord on the Net | a line back to where you jacked in |
| Screen effects | in the app: SOFT, CRT or OFF |

`←→` or `space` changes a setting. Settings last until you close the game.
