# Getting started

Strata Zero is a roguelike set in a city built in layers: the undercity, the street, the skyways, the heights, and the Net over all of them. You play a runner with a flat, a rig and not much else. You get by on the work you take, the systems you crack and the trouble you stay out of.

This manual covers how to play. It doesn't tell you the story.

## Two ways to play

The game comes as an app for Mac, Windows and Linux that opens in a window of its own, and as `strata`, a program you run in a terminal. They are the same game with the same keys, and they keep their saves in the same folder, so a run started in one carries on in the other.

- **In a terminal**, the window needs to be at least 100 columns by 30 rows, and the terminal should show full colour. If it is too small, the game says so and waits for you to make it bigger.
- **In the app**, the window is sized for you. It can add a soft glow or an old monitor's scanlines (SETTINGS, in the pause menu).

### The window's own keys

The app keeps a few keys for itself. Everything else is the game's.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Cmd+=` `Cmd+-` | bigger or smaller text |
| `Cmd+0` | text back to its usual size |
| `Cmd+Ctrl+F` | fullscreen |
| `Cmd+Q` | quit, saving first |

On Windows and Linux the text keys use `Ctrl` instead of `Cmd`, and `F11` or `Alt+Enter` toggles fullscreen.

## The title screen

The game opens with a short splash; any key skips it. Then comes the title, with its menu:

- `CONTINUE` picks up your most recent run where you left it. It is only there when you have a run going.
- `NEW GAME` starts a new runner in a new city.
- `LOAD GAME` lists every run still going.
- `HALL OF FAME` remembers the runners who died for good.
- `MANUAL` opens this booklet.
- `QUIT` closes the game.

Move with `↑↓` and choose with `enter`. On the title, `esc` or `q` quits straight away.

## Your first night

1. Choose `NEW GAME` and pick who you are tonight: one of two ready-made runners, or one you build yourself. Your runner covers this.
2. Read the city's broadcast, then press `space` to jack in.
3. You wake beside your bed in your own flat, at ten at night.

From there the city comes to you. Calls ring in, mail lands on your rig, and the right-hand panes always show what you are doing next. When you want to know what a key does, press `?`.

> Lost? Press `?` for the keys, then `space` to open this manual at its Controls chapter.

## Finding your way around this manual

The manual opens at your desk, its cover on your screen. Press `space` to lean in and read. The contents are on page 1: `↑↓` picks a chapter and `space` turns to it. While reading, `← →` turns the page, `[ ]` jumps a chapter at a time and `c` takes you back to the contents. `esc` leans back from the screen, and `q` closes the manual. It opens from the title screen, from the pause menu, and from the `?` key list.
