# Time and rest

The city keeps time whether you move or not. A minute of game time passes every three seconds, so an hour is three minutes and a whole day a little over an hour. Your first night starts at ten in the evening.

## When time runs

- **On the map**, the clock runs in real time. Standing still costs as much time as walking.
- **Under a menu, the deck or any window**, time stops. Take as long as you like in the deck.
- **On the overworld**, the clock runs, and every step across the country costs time as well.
- **On turns**, the city's clock keeps running while the fight is settled.

## Day and night

Lamps come on at six in the evening and go off at six in the morning; the top band shows a moon or a sun. People keep their own hours, and so do fixers and ripperdocs: from six in the evening until four in the morning. Some work only gets done at night.

## Sleeping

Your own bed, in your own flat, is where you sleep. Stand next to it, press `space`, and choose `i Interact: Sleep`. A timeline shows when you'll wake.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `← →` | wake an hour earlier or later |
| `H` `L` | ten minutes earlier or later |
| `space` | lie down |
| `esc` | not now |

It suggests seven in the morning if you lie down at night, eight hours on otherwise. You can sleep anything from ten minutes to twenty hours.

Asleep, time runs fast. A fight, getting hurt, a call, a scene or someone coming into the flat wakes you, and so does `esc`. When you wake, your HP and FOCUS come back by how long you slept: six hours restores them completely. The game saves as you get up.

You can't sleep with trouble already on you.
