# Your runner

Every run starts with `NEW GAME` and the question "who are you tonight". You can take one of two runners already made, or build your own from nothing.

- **VEX** is a crèche kid behind the ICE: a netrunner raised in the towers, with a deck and the programs to use it.
- **KESSLER** came into the city in a freight can: a solo, all body.
- **CUSTOM BUILD** walks you through every choice yourself.

A ready-made runner opens on the finished sheet, where you can still look it over, go back and change anything, or give it another name.

## Building a runner

A build takes six steps. `enter` goes on to the next, `esc` goes back one, and the footer shows which step you are on.

| Step | What you choose |
|---|---|
| ORIGIN | where you were made |
| PROFESSION | what you did before |
| ATTRIBUTES | what your body can do |
| SKILLS | what you know |
| NAME | what they call you |
| SUMMARY | the finished sheet |

### Origin

Your origin makes two attributes cheap to raise and two dear.

| Origin | Cheap · dear |
|---|---|
| SLAB RAT | BODY, COOL · TECH, NET |
| CRÈCHE | TECH, NET · BODY, COOL |
| STOWAWAY | BODY, REFLEX · NET, TECH |
| VAT-GROWN | NET, TECH · REFLEX, COOL |

### Profession

Your profession sets the money and the kit you start with, and makes one attribute cheap and one dear.

- **NETRUNNER**: `¤320`, a pawnshop deck with the Crack, Copy and Attack programs, and a few odds and ends.
- **FIXER**: `¤600`, an SMG, mirrorshades, the Spoof program and things to trade.
- **SOLO**: `¤250`, an SMG, body armour, a monowire shiv and two stim patches.
- **TECHIE**: `¤300`, a tool roll, spare servos and coils, and a schematic.

### Attributes

There are five: **BODY**, **REFLEX**, **TECH**, **COOL** and **NET**. Each starts at 3 and you have 20 points to raise them, up to 8. A point costs 2, or 1 when your origin or profession makes the attribute cheap, or 3 when it makes it dear. Press `→` to buy a point and `←` to take it back.

- BODY decides how much you can carry, and with COOL how much chrome your body takes.
- REFLEX is your aim with a gun, and how soon you act on the street.
- NET is the power of your programs, and how soon you act on the Net.

### Skills

Skills come in categories: NETRUNNER, WRENCH, IRON, EDGE, GHOST, STREET, RIPPER and MEAT. You have 600 SP to spend. Buy a category first; it brings its first skill with it, and opens the rest of the category to buy. `→` unfolds a category, `←` folds it, `space` buys what the cursor is on.

### Name and perma death

Type a name of up to 16 letters, numbers, spaces and `- _ . '`. Under it is **perma death**, on unless you turn it off. With perma death on, the run ends the first time your runner dies. With it off, dying sends you back to your last save. Saving and dying has more.

## Jacking in

From the sheet, `enter` builds the city and plays its broadcast. `space` shows the whole text at once, and `space` again jacks you in. This is your last chance to change your mind about perma death: `p` turns it on or off for a new run. Once the run has started, it stays as you set it.

## Growing

Your runner earns XP. Each level brings 100 SP for skills and a talent point, and every third level an attribute point too. You spend them all on the deck (The deck covers its SKILLS, ATTRIBUTES and TALENTS tabs). Nothing you buy can be sold back, so choose with care.
