# Chrome and drones

Chrome is the machinery you put into your body: optics, a neural port, arms that hit harder, an adrenal pump. It gives you abilities and raises your numbers. It goes in, and comes out, only in a ripperdoc's chair.

## Finding a ripperdoc

Ripperdocs work out of the back of other businesses: a noodle bar, a laundromat's cellar, a dentist, a tattoo parlour, a butcher. The deck's MAP lists them under ripperdocs, marked `+`, and you can track one there.

A doc keeps the same hours as the city's fixers, from six in the evening until four in the morning. Out of hours the clinic is shut and the door tells you when it opens.

## In the chair

Stand beside the operating chair and press `space`, then `i Interact: Sit in the chair`. The deck opens on ATTRIBUTES, on the list of your body's sites:

- the head: NEURAL, EYES, EARS, JAW
- the body: SPINE, BACK, ORGANS, SKIN
- the limbs: L ARM, R ARM, LEGS

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `↑↓` | move between the sites |
| `space` | what in your pack fits here |
| `space` | in the list: put it in, or swap |
| `x` | take a piece out (press twice) |
| `esc` | get out of the chair |

Chrome arms have sockets of their own for a hand and a forearm.

### What it costs

The doc charges 12% of a piece's value to put it in and 4% to take it out, never less than `¤10`. A swap pays both. If you can't pay, the doc says what it would cost and what you have.

### How much you can take

Your body can carry only so much chrome. Its **load** is twice your BODY and COOL added together, plus six, counting the attributes you built, not what gear adds. The ledger shows how much is used. The doc won't fit what your body can't take.

Every piece of chrome also lowers your EMP resistance a little.

## Drones

A drone cradle, fitted at your BACK by a doc, gives you a bay for a drone. Drones need no doc: dock one in the bay from the chrome list (`space`), or stow it in your pack (`x`), anywhere.

- The **Scout** gives you its scouting ability.
- The **Mule** carries eight kilos more.
- The **Lamp** lets you see three tiles further.

A docked drone comes out beside you, follows you everywhere, and waits with your body while you're jacked in. In a fight it takes its own turn, keeping close to you.
