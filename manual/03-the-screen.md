# The screen

On foot, the screen has four parts: a band across the top about you, the map, a column of panes down its right side, and four lines along the bottom.

## The top band

On the left: your handle, your level and your money in credits (`¤`). Under them are your bars:

- **HP**: green while you're well, amber when you're hurt, flashing magenta when you're close to dying.
- **FOCUS**: what the Net takes out of you.
- **XP**: how close the next level is.

When mail is waiting you see `▣ NEW MAIL` beside your money.

On the right: where you are, and the time, with a moon (`☾`) from six in the evening to six in the morning and a sun (`☀`) the rest of the day. Under them:

- **CARRY**, the weight you have on you against what you can take. It turns magenta when you carry too much, and then you can't pick anything else up.
- How exposed you are: how much light you stand in, and how many cameras see you. On the Net it says whether you are hunted.
- The panes, `[F1]Here [F2]Jobs [F3]Map [F4]Log`, a `*` beside each one that is open.

## The map

You are the `@`. People are `@` too, coloured by what they do: fixers amber, door muscle red. On a run from your rig, an extra line under the top band shows your route, and any trace following it home.

New mail shows a moment as a pill in the map's top right corner, with who it's from and what about.

## The panes

`F1` to `F4` open and close each pane; `v` lists them in a popup too. When the column runs out of room, the log gives way first, then the map.

- **HERE** lists what is on your tile and next to it: a way up or down, things you can pick up, anyone hunting you.
- **JOBS** shows what you are tracking, where the story stands and the job you've taken, each with its next step.
- **MAP** is a close map round you. Outdoors it shows the streets near you and the corners of the main view. Indoors it shows the floor you're on, and a strip of the building's floors with you and the one you're heading for marked.
- **LOG** is the city talking to you: what you did, what you heard, what went wrong. Warnings start with `!` in magenta and the story's lines with `▸` in cyan. `m` opens the whole log to scroll back through.

### Marks on the close map

| Mark | Means |
|---|---|
| `@` | you |
| `▪` | where you jacked in |
| `≡` | stairs |
| `⇅` | a lift |
| `╫` | a crawl's grille |
| `⌂` | your front door |
| `⌖` | the floor you're tracking |

## The bottom lines

- The first says where you are: `on foot`, or `⚡` and the place when you're jacked in. It flashes `:: SAVED` when the game saves. Jacked in, it carries your trace, any alert, and your shield and mask.
- The second tells you what the game wants from you right now, and the keys for it.
- The third follows a fight or a run in progress: whose turn it is, and how a program is getting on.
- The last holds your abilities on `1` to `5`, or your programs when you're jacked in. On turns it shows the moves and action points you have left.

## Seeing the Net from the street

`d` lays the digital overlay over the map: the Net's nodes, the lines between them and the outline of each subnet, drawn over the streets they run under. Press `d` again to take it away.
