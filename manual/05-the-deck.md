# The deck

`tab` opens the deck: everything about your runner, your gear and your work, in eight tabs. The city waits while it is open; time only runs on the map.

## Getting around the deck

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `q` `e` | the tab before or after |
| `1-8` | straight to a tab |
| `↑↓` | move in a tab |
| `space` | act on what's under the cursor |
| `tab` `esc` | close the deck |

The line under the header tells you what your last action did, or why it couldn't. The header itself shows your money, your HP and the weight you carry. The deck opens again on the tab you left it on.

While you're jacked in, your gear and your pack are locked: your hands are back on the street.

## SKILLS

The skill categories, each folded over its skills. A filled `◆` is yours, an empty `◇` is one you can afford, a dim one is out of reach. The card on the right says what the skill does and what it costs.

`←` folds a category and `→` opens it. `space` on something you don't own offers to buy it. A category comes before its skills; buying it gives you its first skill free. Some categories grant an ability, and WRENCH opens the workbench.

## ATTRIBUTES

Your character sheet. On the left are your five attributes, with any unspent points beside them; your secondary numbers (HP, focus, armour, sight, carry, moves), which your gear and chrome raise; and your resistances to each kind of harm. Under them, the number the cursor is on is explained: where it comes from, and what it feeds.

`space` on an attribute spends a point on it. `→` past the last tile crosses to the chrome in your body, which only a ripperdoc can change (Chrome and drones).

## GEAR & INVENTORY

On the left is what you wear: HEAD, EYES, TORSO, LEGS, a PRIMARY and a SECONDARY weapon, and your DECK. On the right is your pack, grouped by kind, with the item under the cursor drawn and described. `→` off the right edge of the rack crosses to the pack, `←` comes back, and `[ ]` filter the pack to one kind of thing.

`space` opens what you can do with the item:

- `e` equip it (an item already in that slot comes off), `u` use it, `i` inspect it, `d` drop one, `D` drop the lot.
- On a slot: a letter puts on something that fits, `u` takes off what is there.

Only your PRIMARY weapon fires. Throwables are thrown from the map, not from here.

### Inspecting

`i` (Inspect / Mod in the item's menu) opens the item's blueprint. On a piece with sockets, `m` switches to modding: pick a socket and fit hardware into it. Hardware only works while it's fitted to something you wear; the Keyjack is the exception, and works from your pack on the fuse in its socket (The city and the world covers it). `s` lets you put stickers on a drawing that takes them; the Keyjack's doesn't.

Your deck's blueprint is where you load programs. `space` on a program lifts it and `space` on a slot drops it in; a deck has only so many slots. The deck's failsafe is under a cover here too (The Net explains it). Change your deck before you jack in, not after.

## WORKBENCH

Once you own the WRENCH category, the workbench shows a drawing for everything you know how to make. `←→` goes from drawing to drawing, `↑↓` through its parts, and `space` builds it when every part is in your pack. The drawing tells you what you're missing.

## JOBS

The story first, then the fixers you know and the jobs they have, then the jobs you've finished, folded away. `space` on a job offers to track it, show it on the map, read its mail or drop it; `t` and `m` do the first two directly. Fixers, jobs and the story has more.

## JOURNAL

What you've learned, building by building: door codes and how you came by them, your keycard, and every backdoor you hold and how long it has left.

## MAP

The city, or the whole world when you zoom out past the city. Move around with the arrows, and hold shift to go eight at a time. `[ ]` steps through places near you, `< >` through kinds of place (fixers, ripperdocs and the rest). `t` or `space` tracks the place under the cursor. `+` and `-` zoom; `f` folds the list of places away.

Jacked in, the map shows two of you. Your self on the Net is the blue `@`, and the cursor starts on it. Your body, where you left it, is the grey `@`.

## TALENTS

Five trees, one for each attribute: MEAT, EDGE, WRENCH, STREET and ICE. You get a talent point at each level. A tree's later rows open as you put more points into it, and a talent may need the one before it at its best. Move with the arrows, `[ ]` change tree, and `space` puts a point into the talent under the cursor. Points can't be taken back.
