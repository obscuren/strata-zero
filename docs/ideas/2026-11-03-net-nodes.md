# What hacking a node means: brainstorm notes (part two of the Net upgrade)

Date: 2026-11-03. Status: **ideas, not a spec.** Written up so the next brainstorm
starts from here. Part one (the Net's look, no light rule, lanes, the cord) is the spec
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-net-look-design.md`. Nothing below is decided
unless it says so.

## Where we landed

- **Every node does something in the meat and something in the run.** A hack should be
  worth it on both sides: change the building or the street, and change the construct
  you're running. Jeffrey: "I love this."
- **Programs get you the node; the node gives the verbs.** Crack (loud) and spoof
  (quiet) take control of a node for a while. What you can then do with it comes from
  the node's kind: a held camera offers tap and loop, a held ad board hijack and decoy.
  Adding a node later means giving it verbs, not writing programs. (Proposed; Jeffrey
  loved the table it came with. Not yet confirmed as the shape.)
- **Lamps: one central node, never a single lamp.** "Individual lamps outside should
  not be hackable, but instead a central node that connects meat space lamps we can make
  go dark." (Decided.)
- **Cameras: see the meat from the Net.** "It would be great if we can see meat space
  from the net (possible?)" Yes, see below.
- **Intercepting packages, for fixers.** To write down and think about.
- **First cut**, if we build in slices: camera, ad board, lamp grid, terminal. They
  are the nodes that do nothing today, and each leans on a system that already exists.

## What exists today

Checked on 2026-11-03 in seed 8:

- Building nodes: door lock 274, ad board 169, server 158, camera 90, terminal 38,
  ICE 13, data vault 8. Street nodes: terminal 15, district hub (backbone) 7, lamp
  grid 7.
- What a landed hack does: a door lock cracked or spoofed opens the building's locked
  doors for an hour; a lift control spoofed makes the lifts answer you; a server copied
  gives a paydata shard and the building's door codes; a vault copied a vault dump; a
  server backdoored keeps its gate yours.
- A camera blinded changes nothing in the world: nothing watches through cameras.
  An ad board or a terminal cracked logs `cracked` and nothing else.
- Runs only work inside a construct. On the open Net nothing can be hacked: the street
  terminals, the lamp grids and the hubs are scenery.

## Lamp grids: one node, a district goes dark

- The generator already makes exactly this: **one lamp grid per district**, anchored at
  the district's first street lamp and linked to the district's hub
  (`citygen/src/generate/net.rs`, `district`). Single lamps are tile features with no
  node; they stay that way.
- **Lights out**: every street lamp of the district switches off for a stretch. The
  light on your tile already feeds how exposed you are (`exposure.rs`), and lamps
  already switch with `lamps_on`, so the dark has a real use on the first day.
- By day the lamps are off anyway: this is a night tool. Neon, holo ads and windows
  stay lit (they are not on the grid), so the dark is patchy, which is nice.
- It is a node on the open Net, so it needs runs outside constructs: the first hack
  that isn't in a construct. Its security is the district's subnet.
- To think about: the whole district or the lamps near the node? A strobe as well as
  off? Does the district raise an alarm, and who comes to fix it? How long?

## Camera feeds: seeing the meat from the Net

**Possible, yes.** A camera node's anchor is a real tile in the meat: building cameras
come from the `Camera` features on the building's footprint at street level. The
renderer can draw any place it has a map for, and the city's sim knows where every
walker is. So a feed is a small live view of the street around the camera's anchor,
people moving in it, drawn while you stand in the Net.

Ways it could look:

- **A feed window**: a small framed view (like the holo call), with a camera's
  dressing: `CAM 03 · KIROFLUX MART · 22:14`, scanlines, a flicker, the odd dropped
  frame. Tap a second camera and it cycles.
- **Only the cone**: the feed shows what the lens covers, not the whole radius; the
  rest of the frame dark.
- **On the minimap**: a tapped camera paints its cone on the street minimap when you
  are back in your body, live for an hour.

To think about:

- Cameras sit on facades, so feeds show the street and the doors, not the floors
  inside. Cameras on floors would be new (floors are generated on demand).
- Does a tap last after you jack out, so your phone or deck keeps the feed?
- Can you mark someone you see in a feed to track them (the track already follows a
  person)?
- Does looking cost anything (heat while the feed is open)?

## Intercepting packages, for fixers

The traffic on the lanes (part one draws it as packets, carrying nothing) becomes real:

- Some packets are **couriers**: a package of data moving between two constructs, a
  corp's own traffic. Most are noise.
- A **fixer job**: "a package leaves Kirosoft for Lumnet at 23:00; I want it." You
  find the route (the job card names both ends; a ping or a tapped hub shows it), get
  ahead of it on the lanes (riding is how), and catch it in transit: a run against a
  moving target, a turn or two while it passes a junction.
- What you do with it: **copy** it (paydata, and the job), **steal** it (it never
  arrives; louder), or **reroute** it (to your backdoor, or to someone the fixer wants
  to frame).
- To think about: what guards a package (an escort ICE riding with it, a timer that
  burns it)? If it lands, is the job lost, or harder (now it's in a vault)? Do couriers
  also walk the meat with physical packages (the sim's walkers)? Is interception a
  fixer-only thing or something you can do on spec and sell?
