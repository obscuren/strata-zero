# Lit sight, occlusion and day/night: riff notes

Date: 2026-10-17. Status: **built** as of 2026-10-18; see
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-lighting-and-time-of-day-design.md` for what
was decided. Written up from a riffing session with Jeffrey about how far the
player can see, and how the city's lights should change that.

## The idea

Field of view blocking is good, and the dark street limiting how far you see
is good. But the city is full of light. A lamp, a neon sign or a lit window
that is in your line of sight should be visible from far away, and so should
the pool of ground it lights. Darkness should limit you; light should not.

In roguelike terms: **a tile is visible when you have line of sight to it and
it is either within your own dark radius or lit above a threshold.**

## What already exists

Checked while riffing, so the next session doesn't have to.

- **FOV** is symmetric shadowcasting (Albert Ford's algorithm) in
  `crates/strata-zero/src/walker/fov.rs`. `compute(origin, radius, blocks,
  in_bounds, mark)` marks every tile in sight within `radius`. It only
  depends on `citygen::Pos`.
- **The radius is fixed per place** in `crates/strata-zero/src/walker/mod.rs`:
  `RADIUS_STREET` 14, `RADIUS_UNDER` 8, `RADIUS_NET` 24, `RADIUS_INSIDE` 30.
  `AppState::sight_bonus` adds `Perk::Sight` from equipment, and two tiles
  between 18:00 and 05:59 for the `lowlight` ability.
- **`Player::update_fov`** runs on every move. It demotes `Visible` to
  `Remembered`, then casts with `radius + sight_bonus` and marks `Visible`.
- **Light is a static grid** of `u8` per stratum, floor and construct,
  computed once at generation. `crates/citygen/src/lighting.rs::compute`
  starts every tile at a base and adds a linear cone per emitter. It
  **ignores walls**: light passes straight through a building.
- **Emitters** (`crates/citygen/src/generate/dressing.rs::emission`):
  lamp (radius 6, strength 120), holo ad (5, 100), neon (4, 90), helipad
  (3, 60), street-level window (2, 30).
- **Base light per stratum** (`dressing.rs::base_light`): Undercity 20,
  Street 60, Skyways 80, Heights 110, Net 0. Interiors use their own
  `BASE_LIGHT` in `crates/citygen/src/interior/layouts.rs`; the Net sets
  `GRID_LIGHT` and `LANE_LIGHT` directly in `generate/cyberspace.rs`.
- **At draw time** visibility is a hard mask (`render/lighting.rs::apply_vis`):
  `Visible` cells are darkened by the light grid through `lit()`, which never
  goes below a quarter brightness; `Remembered` is dim and static; `Unseen` is
  black. Emitting features keep their own colour (`render/tileset.rs`).
- **Night is cosmetic today.** The clock starts at 22:00 and the base light is
  dim, but the radius constant and the light grid are the same at noon. The
  hour only feeds the people overlay and the lowlight perk.
- **Other readers of `Visible`:** the people overlay, guard "seen" checks in
  `crates/strata-zero/src/net.rs`, item placement in `render/items.rs`, and
  the memory map test in `render/memmap.rs`, which asserts that fewer than a
  quarter of the screen is lit for a fresh spawn.

## 1. Lit sight (first)

The core change, about twenty lines in `update_fov`.

- Cast with a **long radius**: a constant such as 40, or the viewport's
  half-diagonal. Shadowcasting over radius 40 touches a few thousand tiles and
  only runs on movement, so cost is not a concern.
- In the mark closure, keep the tile only when **either** its distance is
  within the old radius plus bonus (the dark radius) **or** `map.light(p)`
  is at or above the stratum's threshold.
- Walls follow the same rule as floors: a lit wall is visible, a dark wall
  beyond the dark radius stays `Unseen`. The shadowcaster still uses it to
  block sight either way.
- A lamp at the edge of your FOV then pulls its whole lit pool into view,
  which is the effect we want. A lit window across a dark plaza shows as a
  lit tile; the dark ground between stays black.

### Threshold per stratum

A flat threshold does not work because base light differs so much.

- Heights base is 110. A flat threshold of, say, 90 would make the whole
  stratum lit and sight effectively unlimited there.
- Use **base plus a margin**, so ambient light never counts and only emitters
  punch through. A margin of about 40 means a street lamp (adds 120 at the
  centre, fading linearly over 6 tiles) lights about four tiles around it.
- The Net has base 0 and its own grid and lane light; it probably keeps the
  plain radius rule until the netrunning specs say otherwise.
- Interiors are already radius 30, so the rule changes little there. Could be
  applied for consistency or left alone.

### Knock-on effects

- More tiles become `Visible`, so people on the street and guards in
  constructs are "seen" from further away. That is correct for the player's
  eyes, but guard logic that keys off `Visible` should be checked.
- Memory fills faster, which is fine.
- The `memmap.rs` test bound on the lit fraction will need relaxing.
- `RADIUS_STREET` changes meaning: from "how far you see" to "how far you see
  in the dark". The constant can keep its name but the doc comment should say
  so.

## 2. Occlusion (second, optional)

Light leaks through walls today, so a tile inside a building beside a street
lamp counts as lit. Line of sight still gates visibility, so the player only
notices it in doorways and at windows. Worth fixing once lit sight is in and
the leak is visible, not before.

- Run the shadowcaster **from each emitter** at generation instead of a plain
  cone. A tile gets the emitter's contribution only when the emitter can see
  it. Walls themselves still get lit (the shadowcaster marks the blocking
  tile), which is what we want for lit facades.
- `fov::compute` only depends on `citygen::Pos`, so it **moves into citygen**
  (`crates/citygen/src/fov.rs`) and `strata-zero` re-exports or imports it.
  `lighting::compute` then takes a `blocks` predicate alongside `emission`.
- Cost is generation-time only: a few hundred emitters per stratum, each
  casting over radius 6 or less. Negligible.
- Windows do not block sight (`Terrain::Window` is not in `blocks_sight`),
  so a street-level window keeps throwing its light onto the sidewalk, and
  light from a lamp inside a lobby would reach the street through the glass.
- Doors block sight, so an open interior lamp would not light the street
  through a door. That is acceptable; doors are closed in the fiction.

## 3. Day and night (third, separate feature)

Real day and night means the light grid changes with the hour. The clean way
is to stop baking the base in.

- **Split the grid:** generation stores only the **emitter contribution**
  (base 0). The stratum's base becomes a function of the hour, added at draw
  time and in the lit-sight threshold check.
- `TileMap::light(p)` becomes `base(hour) + emitted(p)`, clamped. The
  threshold check in `update_fov` then compares `emitted(p)` directly against
  the margin, which is simpler than base plus margin.
- **Base by hour**, per stratum: a curve from the current night value (Street
  60) up to a day value (Street maybe 160), with dawn and dusk ramps around
  06:00 and 18:00 to match the existing night window.
- **Dark radius by hour** can go the same way: a day radius large enough that
  the viewport is the limit, a night radius of 14. Or leave the radius alone
  and let the threshold do the work, since by day most tiles pass it anyway.
- The Undercity never sees daylight; its curve is flat. The Net has no sun.
- Emitters could switch off by day (lamps, not neon), which is a flag on
  `emission` per hour rather than a second grid.
- Saves carry the light grid today (`save.rs` checks its size), so the
  split is a save-format change.

## Order of work

1. Lit sight with a per-stratum threshold. Look at it on the street at night
   before deciding anything else.
2. Occlusion, only if the leak shows.
3. Day and night, as its own spec, since it touches generation, saves, the
   renderer and the people sim's idea of night.

## Open questions

- Should a lit tile beyond the dark radius be drawn at full brightness, or
  slightly dimmer than the same tile inside the radius, to read as "far"?
- Do NPCs get the same rule? A guard under a lamp can be seen from the dark,
  but can they see the player standing in the dark? That asymmetry is the
  whole stealth game and probably wants its own riff.
- Does the lowlight ability become "a lower threshold" rather than "two more
  tiles"?
