# The Net upgraded (part one) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** No light rule on the Net, the B "relief" look on the open grid and inside
constructs, a construct card and a Net minimap in the sidebar, lanes that carry you,
packets on the lanes, the cord (switchable), terminals in their own colour, and Signal
ghost given a job again.

**Architecture:** Sight: the walker marks every tile of a Net map visible. Look: a new
`render/netlook/` module paints the whole map area for Net places as the first overlay
(the base pass stays and is overdrawn), from the map plus a small `NetLookView` of
state (tick, tracked building, ICE watch); everything else draws on top as today.
Ride, cord and the ghost's next steps are their own small modules with one overlay
each. The sidebar gets the card lines from `net.rs` and the minimap Net colours from
`netlook`.

**Tech Stack:** Rust, ratatui, crossterm; tests with `TestBackend`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-net-look-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Build into `--target-dir target/smoke`, never `target/`.
- Every check: `cargo fmt --all --check`; clippy `-D warnings` with and without
  `--features strata-zero/window`; `cargo test --workspace` with and without the window
  feature.
- Colours are exact `Rgb`; no named ANSI colours.
- No glyph outside what the fonts carry (`every_drawn_character_has_a_glyph`); the new
  glyphs used are `┄ ┆ ░ ▣ ◈ ⌗ ◇ ◆ ⬡ ▯ ·` and box drawing.
- Tiers: open below 100, guarded 100–159, ICE 160–229, black 230 and up.
- Colours: signal (0,255,200); open (63,168,143); ICE blue (159,230,255); black red
  (255,40,72); amber (255,176,0); terminal violet-blue (140,150,255); void (1,2,4);
  ICE grey (200,208,220); home green (124,255,154); cyan (0,229,255); magenta
  (255,0,200).
- Wording in the UI lower case except names in caps on the card and plates, as the
  mocks show.

## Review Focus

- **A construct partly off screen**: rings, plates and labels are worked out from the
  whole footprint, not the visible part; a label cell off screen is skipped, never
  shifted. Test in Task 2.
- **A construct too small for a plate** (3×3, 4×2 footprints): no ring, no label, no
  panic. Test in Task 2.
- **Map edges**: the void past the Net's edge and around a construct is painted, not
  left as the base pass's remembered backdrop. Test in Task 2 and Task 4.
- **A ride that starts on a gate or next to a node**, and a ride pointed off the lane:
  one step or none, never a loop. Tests in Task 6.
- **The cord with a route whose relay gate is gone** (building without a gate) or a
  player inside a construct: no cord, no panic. Test in Task 7.

## As built

Where the build differs from the tasks above (the spec's "Calls made" has the why):

- `plate::layout(plate: Rect, label, blocked)` takes the plate rect, not the building;
  labels need a plate at least 4 wide (a 3-wide one would read `KI…`).
- Tasks 2–4 landed as one commit; the card's tier words live in `net::tier_word`.
- `RIDE_TICKS` is 1 and `Ride` lives in `src/ride.rs`; a ride stops on the lane tile
  before a construct's gate and on a hub's gate.
- ICE keeps today's colours (white, magenta grey, red black) via `netlook::ice_colour`;
  the watch tints per grade (`inside::watch_tint`). The tracked building is amber only
  on the open Net.
- The card is three lines on the open Net and two inside; HERE's cap is 10 on the Net.
- The fire cursor (`enemies_in_sight`) and program reach (`piece_in_reach`) use
  `line_clear_in` in constructs.
- The Net minimap's pixel priority is lane, outline, inside, hub, void; lanes at 0.6.
