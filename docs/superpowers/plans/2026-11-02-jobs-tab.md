# The JOBS Tab Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the deck's JOBS tab with the agreed design. The list on the left holds
the story, then the fixers with their jobs. The card on the right draws the selected job
as a route, with the window, the brief and a clear track line. Add offer, fixer, closed
and story cards, space verbs, and one set of job words across the game.

**Architecture:**
- The tab moves into its own module, `screens/jobs_tab.rs`, wired into `Deck` like
  GEAR & INVENTORY. It owns its entries, cursor, folds, the cache of walks, its keys and
  its drawing.
- The cards are pure functions in `jobs/card.rs` (and the story's in
  `missions/ui.rs`). Each returns `Vec<Line<'static>>` for a given width, from the state
  and the tab's cached walks.
- The model gains `Job.closed` and `Mail.job`. The objective texts and the shift
  helpers live in `jobs/`.

**Tech Stack:** Rust, ratatui, crossterm; the deck's chrome (`ui/chrome.rs`), popups
(`screens/popup.rs`), the track's routing (`track/`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-jobs-tab-design.md`. The mock is
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-jobs-tab-mock/round2_*.png`.

## Global Constraints

- Exact `Color::Rgb` only, from `ui/chrome.rs` (CYAN, MAGENTA, DIM, FAINT, FAINTER, TEXT,
  RULE, PANEL_BG, BAR_BG), `ui/hud.rs` (AMBER, DIM_AMBER), DONE `Rgb(90, 200, 140)` and
  WHITE `Rgb(255, 255, 255)`.
- Every drawn glyph passes `every_drawn_character_has_a_glyph`. The new ones are
  `▮ ▯ ┿ ┊ ┃ ╋ ┄ ◆ ◇ ▲ ⌖`.
- From 100 × 30 up, nothing overflows the list or the card, and the hint fits.
- Every card line is backed in PANEL_BG across the card's width; the map shows only
  below the last line.
- The job words are spec §10's, verbatim: "take the job", "take the shard" / "shard
  taken", "copy its server" / "server copied", "copy its vault" / "vault copied", "plant
  a backdoor" / "backdoor planted", "deliver the package" / "package delivered", "hand it
  in" / "handed in", "READY", "on the corner till", "off the corner from", "refuses you"
  / "cold" / "new" / "known" / "trusted" / "inner circle".
- "hand in by" appears nowhere. The fixer's hours are advice: a fixer takes the hand-in
  wherever you meet them.
- Hand-in objective: "Hand it in to {fixer} · {district} corner · {start}–{end}".
  Deliver's: "Deliver the package to {name} · {place} · {hours}". Plant's unplanted
  reason: "the backdoor is not planted yet".
- Hint: "Q/E tabs · ↑↓ select · ←→ fold · space verbs · t track · m map · tab close".
- Build and test only into `--target-dir target/smoke`. Before the end, run all five
  checks from CLAUDE.md, plus the Linux and Windows `cargo check`.
- Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. **The shift wrapping past midnight.** At 23:30 a 18:00–04:00 fixer is on shift, with
   4h 30m left. At 05:00 they're off, back at 18:00. A job due 10:00 taken at 22:00
   gets dashes from 04:00 and "off the corner from 04:00".
2. **A fixer off the corner for the rest of the window.** At 05:00 with a job due 10:00,
   the whole remaining bar is dashes and the line says "off the corner now · back at
   18:00, after it's due". READY still shows when the job is ready: the fixer takes it
   wherever you find them.
3. **A walk that can't be made.** Jacked in, or no way from here: rows show "jacked in" /
   "no way from here" in place of a distance. Nothing panics, and `t` still reports the
   track's own refusal.
4. **A mail list holding story mails from a fixer who is also a story role.** Read
   Mezz's mail shows only mails with `job: Some(_)` of Mezz's jobs. "a favour" and the
   news mails never show, and stay unread.
5. **Rows vanishing under the cursor.** A job dropped through the verbs, a job going late
   while the deck is open, or a fold closing on the selected closed row: the cursor
   lands on the next selectable entry, and never on a header or a next-step line.
