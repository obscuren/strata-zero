# Level up and the lock-on: plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-level-up-design.md`. One branch, three steps,
each tested; checks for both front ends at the end.

## 1. The two effects

- `game::Fx`: add `Upload` and `Lock` (unit variants; `Lock` is what content names).
- `render/fx/flourish.rs`: `upload(inst, e, origin, area, buf)` and
  `lock(inst, e, origin, area, buf)`, ported from the mock's `beam` and `reticle`
  (frame = `e / 2`, held at the last frame through the hold). Words from `inst.label`:
  Upload's whole label after `▲ `; Lock's first line bold magenta, second amber.
  Labels flip left when they'd run off the view.
- `FxOverlay` dispatches both; `FxInstance::until` gives both
  `FLOURISH_TICKS` = (18 + 8) × 2.
- Exhaustive matches elsewhere (`actions.rs` landing times, `runs`) get the arms they
  need.
- Tests: Lock's brackets start far out and sit at (±2, ±1) from the lock frame; its
  readout types out; Upload's beam reaches you by frame 6 and its label types out;
  both flip left at the right edge; `until`.

## 2. Upload on a level

- `AppState.levelled: Option<u8>` (UI, bound `_` in `runs::snapshot`): `gain_xp`
  sets it to the new level when it gains any.
- `MapScreen::update` takes it and pushes an `Fx::Upload` instance on your tile and
  place, labelled `LEVEL n`, started now.
- Tests: a gain sets it; two levels at once keep the last; the map's update turns it
  into one instance on your tile and clears it; it waits while another screen is on top.

## 3. Lock on Deadeye

- `flavor/content.ron`: Deadeye's `fx: [Lock]`.
- `casts::mark`: for `Fx::Lock`, push an instance on the foe's tile, labelled
  `MARKED\n<name> · +<bonus>% to hit`. `Mark` stays handled for any ability that keeps it.
- Tests: the Deadeye test asserts a `Lock` on the foe's tile with that label.

## Finish

- `screens/shots.rs`: `flourish_shots` (Upload and Lock mid-play on the street).
- Manual: chapter 02 (a level plays the upload), chapter 07 (Deadeye's lock-on).
- fmt, clippy and tests plain and window, the Linux check; a review; land on main.
