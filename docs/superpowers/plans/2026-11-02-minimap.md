# The minimap Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the sidebar's whole-city colour smear with a close up of the deck MAP
outdoors and the floor plan with a lift-shaft strip indoors, and drop the deck MAP's
seen/unseen shading.

**Architecture:** The deck MAP's pixel colouring moves to one shared module in `render/`
that both the deck MAP and the minimap draw with. The minimap widget is rewritten to draw
a close up (strata, the Net grid) or a plan (floors, constructs) from data it is handed;
`track/` works out what of the track the minimap shows, and `explore.rs` hands it over
through `SidebarView`.

**Tech Stack:** Rust, ratatui cells and half blocks, the `citygen` city and interiors.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-minimap-design.md` (read §1–§8 and the calls
before each task; the section numbers below are the spec's).

## As built

Built inline, task by task, on `claude/minimap-redesign-mockups-d184be`. Where the build
left the plan:

- `stratum_px` and `plan_px` return `Option<Rgb>`, `None` where nothing is; the deck MAP
  fills it with `PANEL_BG_RGB`, the minimap with `StylePack::background`.
- `Through::Stairs` gained `to: i32`, and `track::floor_wanted` reads it, so the strip
  marks the floor the stairs go to (the builder check's first question); a test,
  `locked_lifts_send_you_down_the_stairs`, covers it.
- The lift wins a cell it shares with the stairs unless the walk ends on the stairs.
- The minimap's `@` is not bold: `walking_into_someone_swaps_places_and_space_talks`
  holds the player to one bold `@`, the main view's.
- `NetFloor` counts as building on the Net grid.
- After the branch review: a plain glyph clears the bold a bold one left (your `@` over
  the target or `⌂`); `plan_axis` snaps, then steps on until you are in view, then clamps
  with the far end rounded up to the grid (a tall floor no longer hides its last rows);
  `room_index` fills from the rooms' rects instead of calling `room_at` per tile; the
  strip keeps your floor, then the wanted one, when even their gap rows don't fit.

## Global Constraints

- Build into `--target-dir target/smoke`, never `target/` (CLAUDE.md).
- Check both front ends before calling anything done: `cargo fmt --all --check`, clippy
  with and without `--features strata-zero/window` (`-D warnings`), and the tests with and
  without the window feature, all with `--target-dir target/smoke`.
- Exact `Color::Rgb` / `Rgb` colours only; every non-ASCII glyph in source must have a
  font glyph (`every_drawn_character_has_a_glyph`).
- Pixel scale outdoors: 3 tiles a pixel; indoors 2 if the floor fits, else 3.
- Colours fixed by the spec: walk mixed 0.75 toward `CYAN_RGB`; view corners
  (230, 230, 240); `@` white (255, 255, 255), not bold; rail = `roof.mix((230,230,240), 0.25)`;
  pit (5, 7, 11); wall = `PANEL_BG_RGB.mix(roof, 0.3)`; corridor = `road.scale(2.1)`;
  open plan = `roof.scale(1.25)`; room = `roof.scale(1.1)`; home = room.mix((124,255,154),
  0.22); shaft selection ground (20, 32, 44); district name = `accent.scale(0.85)`.
- Comments and doc comments match the surrounding code: short, plain, in the code's own
  words ("you", "the walk", "a floor").

## Review Focus

- A city edge: standing at x = 0 or the last row must draw without panicking, with the
  panel ground beyond the stratum (Task 4 test `close_up_at_the_edge`).
- A floor bigger than the pane at 3 (the squeezed 100 × 30 pane, 5 map rows, on a
  megablock): it must follow you and never show `@` outside the pane (Task 5 test
  `a_big_floor_follows_you`).
- A track whose target is on another map, or that has no leg: the bottom row must still
  read sensibly (`⌖ lift to L`, `⌖ no way`) and nothing is pinned to the border (Task 3
  tests `right_side_cases`).
- A building with one floor, or with a basement only below L: no strip, or a strip whose
  rows stay inside the pane (Task 5 test `shaft_rows_choose`).
- Tiny areas: a 1 × 1, 2 × 1 and 40 × 2 minimap never panic (Task 4 test
  `minimap_fits_any_area`).
