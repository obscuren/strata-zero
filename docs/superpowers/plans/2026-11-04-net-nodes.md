# The Net upgraded, part two: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Held nodes with verbs (camera tap/loop, ad hijack/decoy, lamp grid lights
out, terminal shell), runs and holds from outside a construct, district traces, camera
eyes in the street, and danger on the open Net (tracers always; sentinels or sweeps).

**Architecture:** An `Owner` (building or district) keys a run's heat; district traces
sit beside building traces. Holds are a node-keyed list in `NetState` with verbs from
the node's kind; a dock (the tether that stays) shows them. Grid ICE are street `Foe`s
with an ICE part, so they see, move, fight and draw on the Net stratum with the foe
machinery. Darkness is a session-only set on the street stratum.

**Tech Stack:** Rust, ratatui, crossterm.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-net-nodes-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Build into `--target-dir target/smoke`; check both front ends (fmt, clippy -D warnings
  with and without `--features strata-zero/window`, tests both ways).
- Nothing new is saved: every new field on a saved struct is `#[serde(skip)]` or lives
  in `AppState`/`NetState` (session).
- Exact `Rgb` colours; magenta (255,0,200) for hijack and grey ICE, red (255,40,40)
  black ICE and camera eyes, green phosphor for the feed.
- Numbers: hold `HACK_MINUTES` (60); outside noise ×1.5 rounded up; reach for verbs 12;
  feed 32×12 (28×10 under 110 columns); camera cone depth 8, 2 heat a game minute;
  lights out 20 game minutes; stare 6 tiles, lure 12; decoy 4 beats; grid fight radius
  20; tracer a tile per 4 ticks; sweep ring every 2 beats, a tile per 2 ticks to 30, 12
  heat; sentinels by district security (<100 none; <160 W; <200 W+G; <230 G+G; else
  G+G+B).
- Verb keys `f` tap, `o` loop / lights out, `i` hijack, `z` decoy, `r` shell.

## Review Focus

- A verb key with no held node in reach: says so, nothing else happens. (Task 3)
- A hold running out while its feed, dock or hijack is up: all of them go. (Tasks 3–5)
- Jacking out mid-run outside, or being dumped: run, feed, trail and tracer cleared;
  sentinels stay for the session. (Tasks 2, 4, 10)
- Grid ICE far away (across the city) never joins a fight or the target list. (Task 8)
- A save after lights out or a hijack: nothing of it in the save; reload shows the
  city lit. (Task 6)
