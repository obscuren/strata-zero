      What makes it a mission
      • SizeSix levels and about three times the hotel's floor. The plaza, three passenger levels, a service level and the old line under it all.
      • Ways inTwo street stairs, the hall's front and west doors, the street lift, the yard's freight lift, the ventilation tower, four tunnels, the sewer through the old line, and the Net from the ad pillar.
      • DepthFive locks from the street to the ledger: the gate line, a staff door, the service level's doors, Line Control's blast door and the core's own ICE.
      • Danger that movesTrains on a timetable, the duty room's guards as reinforcements, a gang under the station that answers to nobody.
      • HeightThirteen links. The freight lift and the ventilation shaft skip floors, the emergency stair only opens at both ends, and the escalators come out a few steps from where they started.
      • Set piecesThe hall over its drop, the gate line, the island platform with a train in, the substation, Line Control, the old platform.
      What it sets up for the metro
      • LinesA line is a stretch of the city's Undercity rail. The game already lays one across every city (verticals.rs); stations stand on it, and a platform's tunnel mouths are the site's exits onto it.
      • TrainsTrains run on a timetable. The track is walkable between trains; a train at the platform is cover, and a way out.
      • RidingRiding is travel between stations: a ride starts and ends on a platform, and a station's platforms are where a job can start or finish.
      • Kinds of stationA stop (one platform, one stair), an interchange like this one, a terminus with sidings and a depot. One type in sites.ron, sized by line count.
      • Line ControlOne per line: the node that holds trains, reroutes them and opens the tunnels' doors. Hacking it changes the network for a while, not just this station.
      • Old linesClosed lines under the new ones: squats, gangs and a way in from the sewer. A kind of level any deep site can have.
