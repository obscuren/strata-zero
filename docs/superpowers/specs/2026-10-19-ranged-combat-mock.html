The burst: three looks

Terminal cells, the game's Combat Zone palette, ticks at the game's pace. The top frame is the aim before you press space; the three below loop one three-round burst each: round 1 hits, round 2 misses and sparks off the wall, round 3 hits. Click the one that feels right; we trim from there.

Aiming

Your turn, act half. hjkl moves the aim cursor over anything in sight; on the goon it reads the shot: name, weapon, distance, the hit chance per round. The footer's right side carries the same readout where the net lists ICE. space fires. (Side panel left out of the frame.)







  

    
A · Tracer

    
Each round is a bright head with a three-cell amber trail, three tiles a tick. Muzzle flash on your tile and the two in front. A hit flashes the goon red and chips fly; a miss runs on and sparks on the wall. Numbers lift off the goon.

B · Streak

    
A round is one streak drawn whole from muzzle to impact, white on the first tick, amber the next, ember the third, gone. Faster and harder; the burst reads as three flashes of a line.
