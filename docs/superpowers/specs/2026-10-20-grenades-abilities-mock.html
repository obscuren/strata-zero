Grenades and abilities

v2: the landing cursor is the net's pick, a cyan block with an × in it. The lot's cells, the game's palette, ticks at the game's pace. Everything loops. Click an option in a section to pick it; we trim from there.

1 · Aiming an area

Same cursor as the shot, but the key you pressed carries a blast radius, so the footprint shades red under the cursor (darker toward the centre) and the readout says what is in it. Cover cuts it: tiles the landing tile cannot see stay unshaded (the two-cell kiosk below the goon). Right: the footprint covers you too, and the readout says so.





The footer's fourth row: abilities and throwables are one numbered list on the act half, ACT; the throwables carry their count. (Rendered at 100 columns.)





2 · The throw


The grenade is a glyph that crosses the line from you to the landing tile, two tiles a tick, then bounces once and settles. Two ways to read height on a top-down map:



  
A · Lob
The glyph rises two rows over the middle of the flight and comes back down: a drawn parabola. Reads as a throw at a glance; costs two rows of honesty on a top-down map.

  
B · Shadow
The glyph stays on the line and grows with height: · o O o ·, brightest at the apex. Top-down honest; reads as the thing's shadow passing.





3 · The blast


Frag, radius 2, dropped a tile short of the goon; the dummy on the edge. Damage falls off by ring: 12 at the centre, 8 at one, 4 at two. The kiosk under the landing tile shadows the two tiles behind it. Numbers lift off everything hit, as tags.



  
A · Bloom
White core, an amber ring that runs out to the edge over two ticks, then smoke that drifts up and thins for a few ticks, and a scorch that stays on the road for the fight.

  
B · Whiteout
The whole footprint inverts to white for a tick, orange the next, then it is over: debris thrown out past the edge and a black scorch under it. Harder and faster; nothing pretty about it.

  
C · Shock (EMP)
The net's blue: rings of ≋ run out from the centre, the glitch tears around whatever has chrome, small numbers, a stun. The EMP's look, and the look a net blast would wear.





4 · Abilities beyond Static


One per stat tree, each with its own look; the streak and the tear (the B and C looks) are reused where chrome is involved. Static stays as it is (hacking, the streak, the tear).



  
Jolt · WRENCH (Tech)
Range 3. The streak in cyan to the first foe, then it jumps a tile to a second one next to them for half. A turn of stun on both. Look: streak, tear, spark, tags on a blue field.

  
Shove · MEAT (Body)
Adjacent. Small damage and the foe goes back two tiles (one if a wall stops them). Look: the red hit flash, a » trail where they were, a plain number. The first melee.





  
Redline · EDGE (Reflex)
No target. Your pools refill this turn; once a fight, long cooldown. Look: the map kicks (the glitch set thinning over three ticks), a cyan pulse out from your tile, + turn lifting off you, the pools flash full in the footer.

  
Deadeye · STREET (Cool)
Marks one foe in sight for the fight: +20% per round at them. Look: the pulsing magenta mark with a ⌖ over their head, the readout's chance lit; then the gun does the rest in its own look.





5 · A fuse, and the goon stepping out


Content decides: a frag goes off on landing (what section 3 shows); a fused thing lies there a turn, blinking, its footprint shaded for everyone to see, and goes off at the top of your next turn. A foe standing in a live footprint spends its moves leaving it if it can. The dummy never moves, so it takes the edge.
