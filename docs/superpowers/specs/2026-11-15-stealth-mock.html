Stealth: the scrambler's veil

An office copy's floor at night, two guards on their rounds. The scrambler hides you behind its interference, and you see the floor through the same interference: the picture splits into its pink and cyan channels. Everything here is built from that split, going in, holding, running low, burning out and switching off. Use the buttons; the phase shows under the screen.


      

        
        
        
        
        
      

      

    

    

      
ONE EFFECT, TURNED UP AND DOWN

      
Every phase is the same drift: the pink and cyan split beside walls and guards, and the wave rolling down the map. Only its strength changes, so nothing jumps.

      
GOING IN

      
The cell catches: the drift sputters in, frames of it flickering over the plain map, wide and wild at first. Then it holds and calms down to its resting split. About a second.

      
HOLDING

      
The split widens to three cells and shivers as the cell drains, and the wave thickens.

      
BURNING OUT

      
The drift keeps going the way it was heading, past empty: the split pulls wider, the wave covers the map and speeds up, the picture slips more often. Then it sputters, the plain map showing through for more and more frames, until it cuts out. The HUD flashes FAILING the whole time. About two and a half seconds.

      
SWITCHING IT OFF

      
The same drift eased out in half a second: the split closes, the wave stops, the veil fades.
