Splash and perma death · round 1

NEW GAME after this change: the chooser, the wizard (perma death set on NAME), the sheet, JACK IN, and then the splash in place of the generation screen. The city builds in the ~35 ms between JACK IN and the splash, so the splash already knows its name. On the splash space jacks in, t ticks DISABLE TUTORIAL, p flips perma death a last time, esc goes back to the sheet. All frames at 120×40 unless marked.

1 · NAME, with perma death

Perma death is a second field under the handle, picked like the title's NET · STREET: the pick in colour, the other grey. ↓ moves to it, ←→ picks, ↑ goes back to typing (space can't flip it: a handle has spaces). It starts on ON; while it's on, the sheet says PERMA DEATH beside the name.

2 · the splash, three ways

Each one: STRATA ZERO, a welcome, [ ] DISABLE TUTORIAL (off), JACK IN, and the red perma death banner along the bottom; it goes grey once perma death is off. The welcome text is the same in A and B; C says it in the city's voice.

A · Marquee

A logo of its own for the splash: STRATA ZERO twice the title's height, cut into three slabs like the strata, magenta running down to cyan. Under it the welcome with the city's name and your handle, the tutorial box and JACK IN, then the banner. Quiet and centred, a title card.
