Drone chrome, round 1

Your Drone cradle on BACK folds out a bay under it in the ledger, the way an arm folds out its hand and forearm. The bay holds one drone; the drone decides what you get (here the Scout drone carries Scout, which the cradle used to grant). The cradle still goes in at a ripperdoc; the drone swaps anywhere. guessdrones weigh in the bag but take no LOAD and cost no EMP: they're not in your body.

The drone on the street: four glyphs

Each lot runs the same walk: the @ stands, the drone drifts within two tiles of you; the @ walks east, the drone trails and catches up; the @ stands again. All drawn in the Scout drone's light (each drone would carry its own). Click the one you like.

A · Rotor

┼ ╳

Two box strokes swapped, a rotor seen from above. Spins twice as fast while it flies to catch up.

B · Blade

─ ╲ │ ╱

One blade turning through four box strokes. Faster on the move.

C · Hover

▀ ▄

Half blocks: the drone bobs in its cell, top half then bottom. Bobs faster on the move.

D · Nav light

• • • ∙ ∙ • ∙

A dot whose lamp breathes bright to dark, with a red tail-light wink once a cycle.
