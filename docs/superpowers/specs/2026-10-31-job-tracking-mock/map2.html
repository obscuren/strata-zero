The deck's MAP · round 2: B with C's exploration

One set of keys, no modes: ←↑↓→ / hjkl roam the map, [ ] step the places list (the cursor jumps there), < > pick the kind, T tracks what is under the cursor (a place, or a spot), + − zoom, f folds the pane. The deck keeps Q/E, the digits and tab. Animated at 30 ticks/s.

speed · tick 0

1 · Pane open · the cursor on a place

The map is C's: a free cursor (the four corners) that you move with the arrows or hjkl, the view panning with it, + and − to zoom (here zoom 2; zoomed out, the whole city fits). The pane is B's: the places of the kind you pick (< >; home and your jobs always show), nearest walk first, scrolled with [ and ], which jump the cursor to the place. When the cursor sits on a listed place, its row lights and opens. T tracks it; the walk glows.

2 · Pane open · the cursor on open ground

Arrowed off into Crown Circle, where you have never been: no row is lit, and the foot of the pane says what is under the cursor. T would track that spot, replacing the clinic's track (one at a time). The kind here is ripperdocs, so the map shows only + (and home, and the job).

3 · Pane folded

f folds the pane to the right: the map takes the deck, the place under the cursor gets C's card instead of its row, and a strip on the right edge marks the folded pane (◂f, PLACES, the kind and how many). f unfolds it. Every key works the same either way.
