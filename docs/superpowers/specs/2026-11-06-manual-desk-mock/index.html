MANUAL · the desk

Opening the manual puts you at your desk, a high floor, the screen in front of a window over the city at night: rain on the glass, neon, the odd car in the air, a brew steaming beside the keyboard. The manual's cover is on the screen. space leans in until the screen is the booklet, with the room still round its edges where there's space; esc leans back. Animated at the game's 30 ticks a second, cells and half-block pixels as the window draws them.
