The overworld in motion · round 1

The atlas look as it is drawn today (round 3's A, round 1's D for the Net), with motion on top: every change is one cell's glyph or colour, picked from the tick and a hash of the tile, at the game's 30 frames a second. Nothing moves by less than a cell. Box and block characters are painted as shapes the way the window paints them; everything else comes from Fira Code. The world is laid out by hand to look like generator output: a coast, a river and its bridge, three highways, Ironwick and the town of Velk, hills, mountains, a few places, you on Route 1.

What the Rust side would need

shared · the clock's light
  • atlas_cell takes the minute: lighting::daylight(minute) and lighting::sky(Street, minute), as the street already uses them. A brightness per kind of tile (ground 0.4→1, water 0.55→1, settlements 0.72→1, open road 0.5→1, settlement road 0.82→1, places 0.85→1) and the dusk cast mixed into ground and water at the same brightness. By day the look is exactly today's.
  • draw_world passes st.tick down the way draw_net_world already does; the screen already redraws every frame and the window repaints only the cells that changed.
A · traffic
  • A RoadAtlas::new(world) built once, like NetAtlas: each route's path from world.routes, both ways, with a cost in ticks per tile (open 12, bridge 14, tile.urban 24) and the running total.
  • Cars are stateless: car c of route r, direction d leaves at c·H + jitter and exists if hash(r, d, c) passes; its tile is a binary search in the running costs. H by the hour (90, 80, 170 ticks). One in five is a two-cell truck at 0.75 of the speed.
  • Short local trips across each settlement's edge (where urban flips along a path), as often as the through traffic at a city and a third as often at a town, so the roads are busiest where they meet a settlement.
  • Drawn as fg/bg overrides on road cells: the head, a truck's body, a red tail and a headlight throw once the light goes.
B · neon
  • A phase and period per district for anim::effects::pulse, quantised to six steps. Urban tiles carry only theme today; the theme is enough as the key, a district id would let two districts of one theme breathe apart.
  • Signs where today's look has them (roll > 0.92), more after dark (0.86–0.92), each with a hashed stutter window. Windows on dense tiles switch on and off in hashed eight-second slots.
  • Beacons: each settlement's densest tiles, picked once and spaced five apart; red ▪ five ticks in 54.
  • The skyline's spill: each tile's distance (≤3) to the nearest urban tile and that tile's accent, computed once per world; water inside it catches reflections.
C · weather
  • Swells: hash(⌊(x − t/30)/3⌋, y + t/120) over the deep sea, each row stepping on its own hashed tick, so they roll toward the coast a tile a second without the whole sea jumping at once; a shore tile (already found in ground_cell) breaks when a swell is one tile out to sea.
  • Rivers in flow order: rivers.rs has each river's path while it generates; keep it on Overworld (or trace the link masks from source to mouth once). Glints are cars with no tail, a tile per 10 ticks.
  • Clouds: two octaves of value noise on the tile grid, moved a whole tile every 45 ticks (each row on its own hashed tick), at three levels of shade; rain under the heaviest with weather::drop_at (at half its speed) and the style's rain glyphs.
  • Wind in the fields: a band of (x + y/2 − t/6) mod 34 brightens ▬. Sea glints by the hour: amber at dusk, the moon's grey at night.
D · lights
  • At dusk a minute per column, minute + (x − 48)·0.8 (the east an hour and a quarter ahead of the west), into the shared light, quantised to eighths, so the shade crosses the map east to west as the clock runs.
  • Lamps on every third open highway tile, each with its distance along its route from the nearest urban tile, so lighting::lamps_on plus that distance turns them on in a wave out of the cities; a hashed one in nine stutters.
  • A beacon pattern per PoiKind, drawn round world.pois: the relay's blink and ring, the checkpoint's flasher and road glow, the truck stop's sign, the water plant's pump, the camp's fire.
NET · in motion
  • NetAtlas's (k + step) % 23 gives way to packets::Traffic, as the city Net runs it: lane = trunk and spur tiles, nodes = the POI nodes and every settlement trunk tile (only the edge ones have lanes, so only they send), SENDERS 1 out here where nodes are few, the pause by the hour.
  • soaks flashes a POI node, or for a settlement the node cells within two of where its trunk enters.
  • Node fields: pulse(tick − d·5, 100), d the distance from the settlement's centre (world.cities[*].centre), so each field breathes out from its core; a rare white flash on ◆.
  • A sentinel on each black node (checkpoint, test range): a ray of 7 tiles turning a sixteenth every 9 ticks (y halved for the cell's shape), the last ray at half; the node pulses as ice_colour(Black). A packet under the beam reddens.
cost
A, D and the Net touch a few dozen cells a frame; B touches the settlements' cells on six brightness steps; C's clouds step every 1.5 s and change a tenth of the map when they do. All of it is per cell, with no per-pixel work.
