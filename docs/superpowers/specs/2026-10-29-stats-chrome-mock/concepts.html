STATS · BODY, REFLEX, TECH, COOL, NET. The tile shows the total; the bottom edge shows what gear and chrome add. Space buys a point.
SECONDARY · HP, focus, armour, sight, carry, move. Points never buy these; only gear and chrome move them. ARM and MOV are new.
RESISTANCES · kinetic, thermal, shock, EMP, toxin, feedback. EMP drops 2 for every piece of chrome you carry. Feedback guards you against black ICE.
LOAD · how much chrome the meat tolerates, from BODY and COOL. Every piece costs load; it's the budget the chrome pane spends.
