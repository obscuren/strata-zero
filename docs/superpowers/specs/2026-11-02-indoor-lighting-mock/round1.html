Indoor lighting · round 1

Indoors today every room gets the same thing as the street: a ● called Street lamp ("Sodium light and a hum you stop hearing"), planted on a fixed six-tile grid whatever the room, lighting a floor that's already at 150 of 255. So a flat, a bar, a corridor and a surgery all read alike, and the lamps sit in the middle of beds and walkways. Below are three directions, relit over the game's own frames (120×40, the map only), each next to today's frame. They share one idea: indoor light has a colour by what lights the room, and rooms go darker so the light shows.

fluorescent · corridors, laundries, waiting rooms LED panel · offices, lobbies, shops tungsten · flats, hotel rooms, bars, back rooms surgical white · the doc's chair neon · a backbar, a stage screen glow · your rig, terminals

A · Ceiling fixtures

The lamp splits into the room's own fixture, drawn where it hangs: tubes ▬▬ along corridors and offices, one bulb • by a flat's bed, pendants over a bar's tables (the table glows), the surgical lamp over the chair. Each throws a smaller pool in its own colour. The city's wear shows: a dead tube (grey) and a stuttering one (dim, it flickers in the game) along a corridor. You can see where the light is, which a later light switch or a shot-out tube can use.

x on a tube: Fluorescent tube · One end flickers. Nobody's coming to fix it.

B · Lit from the walls

Nothing hangs in the room. Light sits in the walls: LED strips in runs along corridors and offices (lit wall segments), a warm sconce in the wall by the bed, a neon backbar behind the counter, the surgery's walls all light. The floor stays clear and rooms read dark in the middle, bright at the edges, which suits the genre. Wall fixtures need no floor space and never sit on a bed.

x on a strip: Light strip · A cold line along the wall, one segment dead.

C · Room tone

No fixtures and no pools. Every room takes an even cast by its kind: cold green-white corridors, blue-white offices, a warm low flat, a white surgery, a dark warm bar. The cheapest to build and the quietest map; A or B could later add their fixtures on top of it.

x on the floor: nothing new; the room's name says what lights it.
