1 · The chair

AThe chair unlocks the ledger

Space at the chair: Sit in the chair. The deck opens on ATTRIBUTES, the ledger in an operating mode: an amber rule, the doc named in the CHROME header, the doc's line where the flavour text sits, esc gets out of the chair. Everything else is today's ledger. Anywhere else (right) the ledger only looks; its hint says why.

CThe doc takes a work order

You talk to the doc, not the chair: a Space window of lettered jobs, PUT IN and TAKE OUT, each with its fee, one key a piece. No pointer, no screen; a swap is a take-out then a put-in. Fast, shop-like, and the least to build.

2 · The clinic

Three hand-laid interiors, each a front business with the chair in back. Fully explored here; the cold-lit tiles are the surgery. ╦ is the chair, green @ the doc, grey @ the front's staff.

ABehind the noodle bar

A noodle bar on the Night Market. The street sees a counter and stools; past the stoves a bead curtain (┆) opens on the back room: the chair (╦) under a cold lamp, a tray, coolers of chrome, a drain in the floor. The doc is the cook's sister.
